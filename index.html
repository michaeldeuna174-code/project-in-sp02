      Safety tips

      Small habits. Stronger protection.

      Three simple actions that make a real difference to your everyday digital safety.

      01

      Lock every account

      Use a unique passphrase for important accounts and switch on two-factor authentication wherever it is available.

      02

      Pause before you click

      Check the sender, inspect the link, and question urgent requests before you act on any message.

      03

      Share with intention

      Review your privacy settings and think twice before posting personal details, locations, or documents.

      When something feels urgent, slow down. Verify unexpected requests through a channel you already trust.
